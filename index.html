Privacy policy.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Bandit Software respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you play Defendypoo.

Information stored by the game

Defendypoo stores game progress on your device. This can include high scores, saved games, gameplay statistics and achievement progress. The current game implementation uses Apple's on-device storage for this information.

Apple Game Center

Defendypoo can connect to Apple Game Center for supported gaming features. If you use Game Center, Apple may process information associated with your Game Center account, such as your player identity, achievements and leaderboard activity, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Bandit Software does not operate the Game Center service.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Defendypoo does not currently include third-party advertising, advertising networks, analytics services or tracking technologies. The game does not use these services to collect information about you.

Sharing and disclosure

Bandit Software does not sell or rent personal information. We do not operate a game server and do not receive the game progress stored on your device. If you use Apple Game Center, information needed for its gaming features is handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Defendypoo does not ask players to create an account with Bandit Software or provide personal information directly to us. Game Center is an Apple service and its use is governed by Apple's terms, privacy policy and parental controls.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bandit Software using the support contact listed on the Defendypoo App Store page.
